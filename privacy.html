Privacy Policy for Xelvo

Effective date: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Xelvo mobile application ("Xelvo", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Xelvo is a local-first media player for playing video and audio files stored on your device and direct media streaming URLs you enter.

1. Summary

Xelvo does not require an account and does not upload your media files. Your videos, music, and other local files stay on your device. Streaming URLs you enter are used by your device to connect directly to the destination address.

We do not sell your personal information. The app uses third-party services (Google AdMob for advertising and Google Firebase for crash reporting, analytics, and configuration) that may collect limited data as described below.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not upload, copy, or transmit your media files (videos, music, subtitles) to our servers or anyone else. The app only reads local files on your device to play them.
  • We do not provide media content, search for media, download videos, bypass DRM, or parse streaming websites.
  • We do not send streaming URLs you enter to our own servers.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

To keep Xelvo free and to improve it, we use the following third-party services, which may collect data automatically.

a) Google AdMob (Advertising)

AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

For users in regions such as the European Economic Area, we present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) allowing you to manage ad personalization.

Learn more:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

b) Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config)

Firebase may collect crash logs, diagnostic and performance data, app usage events, and device identifiers to help us fix problems and understand how the app is used.

Remote Config is used to adjust app settings (such as ad frequency) without requiring an app update.

When playback fails, we may log non-identifying technical playback details such as the error code, container, codec, resolution, source type, and whether another installed player is available. We do not log file names, folder paths, or streaming URLs for this purpose.

Learn more:
https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

These services act as independent data controllers/processors for the data they collect. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

4. Streaming URLs and Network Connections

If you enter a direct streaming URL, your device connects directly to that URL to play the media. We do not send the URL to our own servers.

The destination server may receive your IP address, device network information, and request details as part of normal media delivery. HTTPS streams are encrypted in transit. If you choose an http:// URL, that media stream may not be encrypted by the provider.

5. Permissions We Use

  • Media / Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android versions): used only to find and play media stored on your device.
  • Foreground Service / Notifications: enables background playback and playback controls from notifications and the lock screen.
  • Internet / Network State: required for loading ads, Firebase configuration, and user-entered streaming URLs.

6. How Data Is Protected

Data transmitted by the third-party services above is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

Media streams use the protocol specified by the URL you choose. HTTPS streams are encrypted, while HTTP streams may not be.

7. Children's Privacy

Xelvo is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to minors under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors under 18.

8. Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • In supported regions, you can manage ad personalization through the consent prompt shown inside the app.
  • You can revoke media/storage permission at any time from your device's app settings. Without this permission, Xelvo cannot list or play local files.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or store your personal data on our own servers.

Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is retained according to their respective policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
