Privacy Policy for Xelvo

Effective date: July 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Xelvo mobile application ("Xelvo", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Xelvo is a local-first media player for playing video and audio files stored on your device and direct media streaming URLs you enter. Xelvo may download optional playback support through Google Play when a file cannot be played by your device's built-in decoder.

1. Summary

Xelvo does not require an account and does not upload your media files. Your videos, music, and other local files stay on your device. Streaming URLs you enter are used by your device to connect directly to the destination address. We do not sell your personal information.

The app uses third-party services (Google AdMob for advertising and Google Firebase for crash reporting, analytics, and configuration) that may collect limited data as described below. Optional extended playback support runs on your device and does not upload your media to Xelvo.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • We do not upload, copy, or transmit your media files (videos, music, subtitles) to our servers or anyone else. The app only reads local files on your device to play them.
  • We do not provide media content, search for media, download videos, bypass DRM, or parse streaming websites.
  • We do not send streaming URLs you enter to our own servers.

3. Information Collected by Third-Party Services

To keep Xelvo free and to improve it, we use the following third-party services, which may collect data automatically:

Google AdMob (advertising)

AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. For users in regions such as the European Economic Area, we present a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) allowing you to manage ad personalization.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy information.

Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Analytics, Remote Config)

Firebase may collect crash logs, diagnostic and performance data, app usage events, and device identifiers to help us fix problems and understand how the app is used. Remote Config is used to adjust app settings (such as ad frequency) without an update.

When playback starts or fails, or when the app switches between installed playback engines, we may log non-identifying technical categories such as the engine used (Media3 or libVLC), result, failure stage, container and codec family, resolution bucket, source type, and handoff outcome and latency bucket. These categories help us measure format support and playback reliability. We do not log file names, folder paths, media titles, streaming URLs, exact decoder names, or exception messages for this purpose.

Learn more: Firebase privacy and security.

These services act as independent data controllers/processors for the data they collect. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

4. Optional Playback Support

For some formats, Xelvo may download optional extended playback support through Google Play. This component uses open-source VideoLAN playback technology. VLC and VideoLAN are trademarks of the VideoLAN non-profit organization.

The optional playback component runs locally on your device. It does not give Xelvo a server-side copy of your media files, file names, folder paths, or streaming URLs. Google Play may process information related to downloading and installing app components according to Google's policies.

5. Streaming URLs and Network Connections

If you enter a direct streaming URL, your device connects to that URL to play the media. We do not send the URL to our own servers. The destination server may receive your IP address, device network information, and request details as part of normal media delivery. HTTPS streams are encrypted in transit. If you choose an http:// URL, that media stream may not be encrypted by the provider.

6. Permissions We Use

  • Media / storage access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older devices): to find and play the video and audio files on your device. This access is used only to play your files locally.
  • Foreground service / notifications: to enable background audio playback and playback controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
  • Internet / network state: to load ads, Firebase configuration, optional playback support through Google Play, and user-entered streaming URLs.

7. How Data Is Protected

Data transmitted by the third-party services above is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Media streams use the protocol of the URL you choose; HTTPS streams are encrypted, while HTTP streams may not be.

8. Children's Privacy

Xelvo is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed to minors under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors under 18.

9. Your Choices

  • You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • In supported regions, you can manage ad personalization through the consent prompt shown in the app.
  • You can revoke media/storage permission at any time in your device's app settings (the app will then be unable to list your files).
  • You can remove optional playback support from Xelvo's settings when it is installed.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or store your personal data on our own servers. Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is retained according to their respective policies. For any questions or requests regarding data, contact us at xelvo.support@gmail.com.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: xelvo.support@gmail.com.
