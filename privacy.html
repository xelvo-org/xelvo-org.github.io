Xelvo legal

Privacy policy

Xelvo is built for media you already have. Local media stays on your device by default, while optional Google services are kept separate from the core playback task.

Last updated: October 9, 2026

At a glance

  • Xelvo does not require an account and has no approved Xelvo backend.
  • Local media is not uploaded by default.
  • Ads, Ad privacy options, usage and crash diagnostics, remote configuration, and purchases use Google services only when their feature, configuration, and consent requirements allow.
  • Android Clear storage or uninstall removes Xelvo's app-local records and settings, not source media.

1. Scope

This policy explains how the Xelvo Android app handles information during local playback, user-requested network playback, advertising, telemetry, remote configuration, Google Play purchases, and support. In this policy, "Xelvo," "we," and "us" refer to the provider of the Xelvo app.

Third-party services and websites have their own privacy practices. Their policies apply when they process information.

2. Information handled on your device

With Android permission or a file selection you make, Xelvo can read local media needed to browse and play it. The app can keep app records such as playback activity and progress, favorites and playlists, settings, local diagnostics, and data waiting for an eligible telemetry request.

Media filenames, titles, folders, paths, URIs, media content, subtitles, lyrics, artwork, search terms, playlist names, bookmarks, and identifiers derived from those values are not sent as Xelvo telemetry. Playback events may include an exact position. A random playback ID may link the start, decoder transition, low-memory decoding notice, and exit of one successful playback; it contains no media information and is not reused for another playback. Local media is not uploaded to an Xelvo server by default, and Xelvo does not currently provide an account or cloud sync.

If you explicitly open an HTTP or HTTPS media address, Xelvo connects to the host you selected so it can request that media. The host and network providers may receive normal connection information such as your IP address. When a stream is retained in Recent, a playlist, Favorites, or the current playback sequence, its URL is stored on your device so Xelvo can play it again. Xelvo removes that stored URL after its last retained reference is removed. Xelvo does not save URL credentials, cookies, tokens, or custom authentication headers.

3. Google services

Availability and collection depend on the released app build, configuration, region, consent status, and the service's own rules. Local playback remains available when these services or the network are unavailable.

Advertising and consent

Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP)

When ads are enabled and a request is permitted, Google may process device or other identifiers, app and ad interactions, approximate location derived from network information, and related technical data to deliver and measure ads, apply Ad privacy options, prevent abuse, and meet legal requirements. UMP provides Ad privacy options where applicable. Xelvo remains fully usable whichever option you choose; ads may become non-personalized, limited, or unavailable.

If a Google ad cannot be requested or is unavailable, the library may instead show a bundled Xelvo message about ad-free options. That local message does not create an AdMob request. When Analytics is enabled, Xelvo may record that the House message was shown or that its Remove Ads action was used; it does not report ad revenue or media identity.

Product telemetry

Firebase Analytics

When Analytics is enabled and consented, Xelvo may report app lifecycle and screen activity, including which Xelvo screen is visible, workflow results, ad impressions, Remove Ads entry use, and closed technical categories such as container, a network stream format declaration, codec, decoder, failure category, and compatibility fallback result. A stream format declaration is mapped immediately from the response Content-Type or URL suffix into a closed category; this format observation does not create, retain, or report a raw copy of the Content-Type, suffix, response headers, URL, host, path, redirect target, or load identity. Device-local stream URL storage is described above and is not sent as Xelvo telemetry. Xelvo may also report integer-millisecond startup time, active-playing time, finite media duration, playback position, video width and height, rounded video frame rate, and audio channel count to improve playback compatibility and performance. When a low-memory device (one that Android reports as low-memory, or that has less than 2 GiB of total memory) cannot hardware-decode a video and Xelvo shows a notice that it will use software decoding, Xelvo may also report coarse device memory information: total memory rounded to the nearest 256 MB, the Android low-memory device flag, and the app memory limit Android assigns. This information describes the device class, not you, and is not used as an identifier. On Android 7 to 9 devices with a removable SD card or USB storage mounted, when Xelvo builds its Folders view Xelvo may also report, at most once per app version and media type, the Android version, counts of mounted removable storage volumes and of those whose folders it could locate, and a fixed outcome category (for example success or a fixed failure reason), to check that removable-storage folders are found correctly; it does not report volume names, identifiers, folder or file names, or paths. Firebase may derive standard visible-screen engagement from screen events. These reports are designed not to include media filenames, titles, paths, URIs, URLs, media content, user-entered text, credentials, or raw error messages. A random playback ID may link start, decoder transition, low-memory decoding notice, and exit events from one successful playback; it contains no media information, is not persisted by Xelvo, and is not reused for another playback. Playback events may also include the exact non-negative playback position at the start, failure, or exit boundary. When Analytics collection is allowed, it uses app-instance or installation identifiers and may use the Advertising ID for acquisition attribution; AdMob may use it for ads. Where required, Google UMP privacy options manage both advertising consent and Analytics storage through Google Consent Mode. Xelvo does not show a second Analytics prompt or keep a duplicate consent record. Outside those regions, Analytics may run as ordinary app operation. With Analytics storage declined, advanced Consent Mode may still send limited, non-persistent consent and activity signals without Advertising ID, cookies, or Analytics persistent identifiers. Xelvo also keeps a global switch to stop collection remotely. Analytics never affects local playback.

Under the same Analytics rules, Xelvo may also report fixed categories and counts that show where the app is giving a poor experience. These use only fixed values and whole numbers:

  • Playback smoothness: for each playback session, how many times and for how long playback paused to buffer, how many seeks you made and the longest wait for one, and how many video frames were dropped.
  • Device and system limits: when Android refuses to let background playback start or keep running, or refuses audio focus; when picture-in-picture or the share sheet cannot open; when the media library cannot be read; and the result of a media permission request (for example granted, partly granted, or denied).
  • App start and previous exit: how long a cold start took to show the first screen, and, on Android 11 and later, the category Android recorded for why Xelvo's own previous app process ended (for example low memory, a crash, an ANR, or closed by the user), whether it was in the foreground or background, and whether the device is a low-memory device. Xelvo does not read or send the system's free-form exit description or trace, and does not read other apps' exits. A crash or ANR exit is sent only when crash reporting is on; turning crash reporting off also withholds it.
  • Subtitles and lyrics: whether loading embedded, picked, or nearby subtitle or lyrics files worked, a fixed format type (for example SRT or LRC), and when you change the subtitle text encoding setting, the old and new setting and whether a subtitle or lyrics file was loaded. The text and file names are never sent.
  • File actions: for a rename, delete, move, or copy you start, the action type, its overall result, and counts of items that failed or were not supported. File names, folders, and targets are not sent.
  • Purchases, ads, and consent: when a purchase you start fails, whether it was a subscription or one-time purchase, a fixed reason, and at which step it failed (cancelling is not reported); when an ad fails to load or show, the ad position and a fixed reason; and the overall result of the Google consent form (for example obtained, not required, or an error).

These reports do not include product IDs, prices, order or purchase tokens, raw error codes or messages, or media information. Some are limited to one report per app launch.

Reliability

Firebase Crashlytics

In production builds, Crashlytics is enabled by default for reliability and security. Google may process crash logs, ANR information, diagnostics, exception types and stack traces, and app or device technical information. Xelvo may attach a small fixed diagnostic set to crash, non-fatal error, or ANR reports: when three classes of local media work began. These fields do not name the media being processed. When playback of a supported file or stream fails because of a problem in Xelvo itself, Xelvo also sends a non-fatal error report under the same crash reporting setting. It contains a fixed failure category, the playback stage, whether the item was local or a stream, audio or video, the decoder route, the container and codec types, and a short list of the last few playback actions and results with their timing. Failures caused by the content or environment, such as a damaged or unsupported file, a network error, or a missing or inaccessible file, do not by themselves create a Crashlytics report, but they can appear as fixed categories in that short list of recent playback actions. The list of recent playback actions may also be sent with a later crash report from the same app session. If the Google Mobile Ads SDK fails to start on a device, Xelvo stops trying to load Google ads until the app restarts and may send one non-fatal error report per app launch containing only a fixed category; a known Google Mobile Ads SDK error caught on its own background thread may be reported with its exception type and stack trace. Under the same setting, Xelvo may also send non-fatal error reports for other problems in Xelvo itself, each grouped by a fixed cause and carrying only fixed values: a playback engine that could not be released cleanly (engine type and cause), the next part of a playback list that could not be read (fixed read result), a media library read error (audio or video), a Google Play purchase that could not be acknowledged (fixed response category) or confirmed (subscription or one-time purchase), an ad-free reward that could not be saved, and app settings or purchase-state storage that was damaged and reset (which store). Xelvo limits explicit reports to sanitized categories and does not intentionally attach media names, paths, URLs, content, user input, screenshots, credentials, or exception details that contain those values, or the system's free-form ANR description. Every user can turn crash reporting off under Privacy & legal → Data collection. The change fully applies after Xelvo restarts, when reports waiting to be sent are deleted. Reports already uploaded cannot be recalled. Xelvo also keeps a global emergency switch.

Configuration

Firebase Remote Config

When enabled, Xelvo can fetch approved app configuration from Firebase. Firebase may handle app-instance and technical app or device information for that request. Remote configuration cannot grant consent, bypass UMP or the Crashlytics user setting, or make core local playback depend on the service; telemetry switches can only stop their corresponding collection.

Purchases

Google Play Billing

Google Play processes subscriptions and one-time purchases. Google may handle payment, purchase history, account, device, and transaction information under its own policy. Xelvo receives the product and entitlement status needed to provide ad-free access; Xelvo does not require a separate account or receive full payment-card details. As described above, Xelvo's Analytics may record that a purchase you started failed, and Crashlytics may record a purchase that could not be acknowledged or confirmed, using only the purchase type and fixed categories.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. Support communications

If you email support, we receive the email address and message content you choose to send so we can understand and respond to the request. Do not send media files, filenames, paths, URLs, subtitles, lyrics, playlists, credentials, payment-card details, or other private content. See the support page for safer diagnostic details to include.

5. Data Xelvo does not use for telemetry

Xelvo telemetry is not designed to collect or report:

  • Media filenames, titles, directories, paths, URIs, URLs, audio, video, subtitles, lyrics, artwork, or screenshots.
  • Artist or album names, search terms, playlist or bookmark names, clipboard content, or other user-entered text.
  • Playback positions other than the approved non-negative position reported at playback start, terminal failure, or exit; complete queues, item-level history, file-operation targets, or full app databases.
  • Passwords, tokens, cookies, authentication headers, exception details containing user information, or unsanitized logs.
  • Android ID, IMEI, MAC address, SSID, or hardware serial number as Xelvo-defined telemetry fields.

6. Ad privacy, usage data, and app storage

Media access
Android permissions and the system file picker control which media Xelvo can access. Revoking access can limit browsing or playback.
Ad privacy options
Where UMP makes them available, Ad privacy options let you review or update advertising and Analytics storage choices. Xelvo remains fully usable whichever option you choose. Depending on your choice, region, and service availability, ads may be non-personalized, limited, or unavailable. A change applies to eligible future processing; it does not promise deletion of provider history. With Analytics storage declined, Google may still receive limited, non-persistent consent and activity signals without Advertising ID, cookies, or Analytics persistent identifiers.
Usage and crash diagnostics
Analytics follows the Google privacy choice described above. Crashlytics is separately enabled by default for reliability, and every user can turn it off under Data collection. Turning it off fully applies after Xelvo restarts and deletes reports still waiting to be sent; uploaded reports cannot be recalled. The Analytics report of why Xelvo's previous app process ended (which can say it crashed or stopped responding) follows the Analytics choice, not this Crashlytics setting. Local playback is never affected.
Android Clear storage and uninstall
Android system Clear storage and uninstall can remove Xelvo's app-local records and settings, subject to Android behavior. These system actions do not delete source media or provider-held service history.
Explicit media-file deletion
A delete operation you explicitly confirm for selected media is a separate device-file action. When Android authorization and the source allow it, that action can delete the selected source media.

No remote deletion service: Xelvo has no account or approved backend and cannot identify or delete provider-held AdMob, Firebase, or Google Play records on your behalf.

7. Retention and deletion limits

Local app records remain in Xelvo's app storage until changed or removed through Android system Clear storage or uninstall, subject to Android behavior. Those system actions remove app-local records and settings, not source media. An explicit delete operation for selected media is a separate device-file action.

Google services apply their own retention and deletion rules. Xelvo does not promise deletion of provider-held information, including information already received that Xelvo cannot identify as belonging to a particular person or device. Use the relevant Google account, service, and privacy controls for provider-held information.

8. Age

Xelvo is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Xelvo's features, service configuration, or release requirements change. The updated page will show a new "Last updated" date. Material differences must be reflected in the app and store disclosures where required.
