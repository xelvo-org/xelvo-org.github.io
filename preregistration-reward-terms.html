Google Play offer

Pre-registration reward terms

These terms apply to Xelvo's Lifetime ad-free support reward for eligible users who pre-register through Google Play.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Reward summary

Eligible Google Play users can receive the Xelvo in-app product Lifetime ad-free support (ad_free_lifetime) without a separate payment. The entitlement has no recurring expiration date.

1. Eligibility

You are eligible only if all of the following apply:

  • You pre-registered for Xelvo through its official Google Play listing while the reward offer was available.
  • Google Play identifies your account and country or region as eligible for the offer.
  • You install and open Xelvo through Google Play within the claim period shown or applied by Google Play.
  • You comply with these terms, the Xelvo Terms of Use, and applicable Google Play terms.

Google Play records pre-registration and determines whether an account qualifies. Xelvo cannot manually create or transfer a pre-registration record.

2. What the reward provides

The reward grants the same ad-free entitlement represented by the Google Play in-app product ad_free_lifetime. Once successfully granted and recognized, Xelvo will not show its advertising placements while that entitlement remains available to the app through the eligible Google Play account.

The reward does not include cash, store credit, a subscription, media content, or ownership of the app. It does not reimburse an earlier purchase or grant future products or services that are separate from the ad-free entitlement.

3. Grant and restoration

Google Play delivers the reward to an eligible account under its pre-registration program. No separate payment is required. Delivery timing can depend on Google Play, account status, country or region, network availability, and the installed app version.

If you reinstall Xelvo or move to another compatible Android device, use the same eligible Google Play account. Xelvo can restore the entitlement only when Google Play reports it to the app.

4. Limits

  • The reward is limited to one grant per eligible Google Play account.
  • It cannot be sold, transferred, exchanged, or redeemed for cash.
  • It is unavailable where prohibited by law or where Google Play does not support the offer.
  • Fraud, abuse, account manipulation, or attempts to obtain duplicate rewards can result in disqualification where permitted by law and Google Play rules.

5. Meaning of Lifetime

"Lifetime" means that the granted ad-free entitlement has no recurring expiration date. It does not guarantee that Xelvo, Google Play, Android, any device, or any particular feature will remain available forever. Statutory consumer rights are not limited by this section.

6. Privacy

Google Play handles the account, pre-registration, eligibility, and reward-delivery information required for this offer under Google's own terms and privacy practices. Xelvo handles information as described in the Xelvo Privacy Policy.

7. Changes and conflicts

The offer may be changed or withdrawn before a reward is granted where permitted by Google Play and applicable law. A reward already validly granted is not given a new expiration date by a later change to these offer terms.

If these reward terms conflict with the general Xelvo Terms of Use, these reward terms control only for this pre-registration reward. Mandatory law and applicable Google Play program rules continue to apply.

8. Contact

For help with Xelvo recognizing a reward that Google Play has granted, contact:

xelvo.support@gmail.com
